# EPISODE 1 — "My Daughter Owned the Bank — But Nobody Knew I Was Her Father"

**Format:** Vertical 9:16 | **Target length:** 60–90 sec

## NARRATION SCRIPT (voiceover)

> I was the security guard at the bank my daughter owned.
> And for three years… she never told anyone I was her father.
> Every morning at 6 a.m., I stood at the gate checking bags and greeting customers.
> Most people barely noticed me. Some didn't even say good morning.
> But I didn't mind. At my age, I was grateful to still have a job.
> Especially after losing everything.
> Ten years earlier, I owned a thriving transport business. Life was good.
> Then one bad investment destroyed everything.
> Cars were repossessed. Properties were sold. Debt swallowed us whole.
> Overnight, I went from employer to job seeker.
> The humiliation nearly killed me.
> But what hurt most was watching my daughter, Amara, suffer because of my mistakes.
> She was only sixteen then. Brilliant. Ambitious. Determined.
> I expected her to hate me.
> Instead, she hugged me and said: "We will rise again, Dad."
> I never forgot those words.
> Years passed. I worked wherever I could. Security jobs. Night shifts. Anything honest.
> Meanwhile, Amara studied relentlessly. Scholarships. Part-time jobs. Sleepless nights.
> She fought for every opportunity.
> Then one day she graduated at the top of her class in finance.
> A few years later, she started a small financial company. Then another. Then another.
> Before long, she owned one of the fastest-growing microfinance banks in the region.
> I was proud. Very proud.
> Then something unexpected happened.
> She offered me a job.
> Not as a manager. Not as an executive.
> As a security guard.
> At first, I was shocked. But jobs were scarce. And I accepted.
> *(Cliffhanger line for end card):* For three years, I stood at the gate of the bank my daughter owned… and nobody knew.
