# AI VIDEO SERIES PLAN
## "The Security Guard" — A 3-Episode Emotional Storytelling Series

---

## SERIES CONCEPT

A viral-style emotional storytelling series (African/Nigerian setting) about a father who lost everything, took a security guard job at the bank his daughter secretly owned, and the day the world found out who he really was.

**Target platforms:** YouTube (Shorts + long-form), TikTok, Facebook Reels, Instagram Reels
**Target audience:** 25–55, fans of emotional redemption stories, family values content
**Tone:** Emotional, cinematic, inspirational
**Setting:** Modern-day Nigeria (Lagos) — bank, offices, modest family home

---

## EPISODE BREAKDOWN

### EPISODE 1 — "My Daughter Owned the Bank — But Nobody Knew I Was Her Father"
**Hook (first 5 seconds):** *"I was the security guard at the bank my daughter owned. For three years… she never told anyone I was her father."*
**Content:**
- Cold open hook (present day at the bank gate)
- Flashback: thriving transport business
- The bad investment — everything lost
- The humiliation of going from employer to job seeker
- 16-year-old Amara's promise: "We will rise again, Dad."
- Cliffhanger: *"Then one day, she offered me a job. Not as a manager. As a security guard."*

**Duration target:** 60–90 sec (Shorts/Reels) or 2–3 min (YouTube)

---

### EPISODE 2 — "Three Years Invisible"
**Hook:** *"Customers treated me like I was invisible. But every evening, my daughter stopped by my post and asked one question…"*
**Content:**
- Daily life at the gate — being ignored, spoken to rudely
- Nobody knows he's her father — not staff, not clients
- The evening ritual: "How was your day, Dad?"
- The wealthy businessman arrives — it's the former partner who abandoned him
- *"So this is what became of you?"* — public mockery
- Cliffhanger: *"An hour later, shouting erupted from the executive floor."*

**Duration target:** 60–90 sec (Shorts/Reels) or 2–3 min (YouTube)

---

### EPISODE 3 — "That Security Guard Is My Father"
**Hook:** *"Then my daughter stood up in front of everyone and said words nobody expected…"*
**Content:**
- The businessman demands a massive loan
- Amara calmly rejects it
- He points at the father: *"That's what failure looks like."*
- The revelation: *"That security guard is my father."*
- The three "The man you're mocking…" lines
- The public hug: *"Everything I am started with him."*
- Staff apologies; the father–daughter closing conversation
- Lesson: *"A person's uniform is not their value."*
- CTA: *"Would you proudly introduce your parents no matter what job they do? Tell me in the comments."*

**Duration target:** 90–120 sec (Shorts/Reels) or 3–4 min (YouTube)

---

## SERIES STRATEGY

- **Post cadence:** 1 episode every 2 days to build anticipation
- **End each episode on a cliffhanger** to drive follows
- **Episode 3 ends with a question CTA** to drive comments (algorithm boost)
- **Recycle:** After the 3-part series, combine into one long-form video (5–8 min) for YouTube
- **Hashtags:** #emotionalstory #storytime #africanstories #nigerianstories #family #fatherdaughter #motivation
