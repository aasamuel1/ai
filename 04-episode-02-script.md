# EPISODE 2 — "Three Years Invisible"

## NARRATION SCRIPT (voiceover)

> For three years, I stood at the gate of the bank my daughter owned.
> Customers knew her as Madam Amara.
> Nobody knew I was her father. Not the staff. Not the clients. Nobody.
> Sometimes employees spoke rudely to me.
> Sometimes customers treated me like I was invisible.
> I never complained.
> Because every evening, my daughter would stop by my post and ask:
> "How was your day, Dad?"
> And somehow, that was enough.
> Then one afternoon, a wealthy businessman entered the bank.
> The moment he saw me… he laughed. Loudly.
> It was my former business partner.
> The same man who abandoned me when my company collapsed.
> He stared at my uniform and smirked:
> "So this is what became of you?"
> People turned to watch. I felt my face burn with embarrassment.
> Then he walked inside, still laughing.
> *(Cliffhanger):* An hour later… shouting erupted from the executive floor.
