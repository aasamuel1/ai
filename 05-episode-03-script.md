## NARRATION SCRIPT (voiceover)

> The businessman was demanding a massive loan.
> Staff tried explaining that approval wasn't automatic. But he kept insisting.
> Then Amara walked into the room. Calm. Confident. Professional.
> The businessman smiled immediately… until she rejected his request.
> His expression changed. Then he pointed toward me through the glass walls:
> "Look at your security guard. That's what failure looks like."
> The entire office went silent.
> Amara slowly stood up. Then she said words nobody expected:
> "That security guard… is my father."
> Silence. Complete silence.
> Then Amara continued:
> "The man you're mocking sold his properties so I could stay in school."
> "The man you're mocking worked night shifts so I could eat."
> "The man you're mocking taught me integrity before he taught me success."
> People started tearing up. Some employees looked ashamed.
> Then Amara walked out of the office, came straight to me, in front of everyone… and hugged me.
> "Everything I am started with him."
> Nobody spoke. Nobody moved. Even the businessman lowered his head.
> That evening, staff members lined up one after another to apologize.
> But I wasn't thinking about them. I was looking at my daughter.
> The little girl who once promised we would rise again… had kept her word.
> Before we left, Amara held my hand and smiled:
> "Dad, I never hid who you were because I was ashamed.
> I waited because I wanted people to learn that a person's uniform is not their value."
> And honestly? That was worth more than any loan, any company, or any fortune I ever lost.
> *(CTA):* Would you proudly introduce your parents, no matter what job they do? Tell me in the comments.
